## LootLog 3.2.0

**Download `LootLog-win-Setup.exe` below and run it.** One click, no admin rights. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed yet: click **More info → Run anyway**.

### New
- **Installer + automatic updates.** New versions download in the background and install by themselves when Fortnite is closed. Never during a match.
- **Step-by-step setup.** The first start walks you through it one step at a time: your chest key, replays, the Fortnite window mode, and the on-screen box.
- **Streamer switch.** Let viewers see the chest box on stream (OBS / Discord).
- **You + your team.** Exact chests for you and for your squad, with chests per match (CPM) for both.
- **Pirate token odds with Treasure Hours.** Chests opened during Treasure Hours count 1.5×.
- **Better live counter.** It catches fast chest opens and learns what chests look like on your screen.

LootLog is a fan-made tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games.
